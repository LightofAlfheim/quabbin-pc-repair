What these prices cover. Each price is for a straightforward job of that kind. Complicated cases, extensive troubleshooting, data transfers and any additional work are quoted separately, and I’ll check with you before doing any of it.

Diagnostic fee. Basic diagnostics is $10, paid when you drop off your computer, and covers an initial diagnosis. If you approve the repair I quote, that $10 is credited toward the repair, so you don’t pay it twice. If you decline the repair, the $10 covers the diagnostic work already done. If I can’t diagnose the problem, I’ll explain why and refund the $10. Finding no obvious fault isn’t the same as being unable to diagnose: the fee pays for the diagnostic work, not a guaranteed discovery of a fault, so if I test it and find nothing wrong, I tell you what I checked and the $10 still applies. Not every problem can be diagnosed for $10: if yours needs extensive troubleshooting, I’ll contact you and we’ll agree on an additional diagnostic charge before I do any more.

Parts and estimates. Prices are for labor only. Replacement parts and materials are listed separately on your quote. These are introductory prices and may change. Your final quote depends on what the repair actually involves, and you’ll see it before any work begins.

Questions people ask

What does it cost just to have it looked at?
Basic diagnostics is $10, paid at drop-off. I tell you what’s wrong and what the fix would cost, and you decide whether to go ahead. If you do, the $10 is credited toward the repair. If you don’t, I keep the $10 for the diagnostic work. If I can’t diagnose it, I explain why and refund the $10. The fee pays for the diagnostic work, not a guaranteed discovery of a fault, so if I test it and find no obvious fault, I tell you what I checked and the $10 still applies. Some problems take extensive troubleshooting to pin down. If yours does, I contact you first and we agree on an additional diagnostic charge before I go further.
Are parts included in those prices?
No, the prices are labor only. If a repair needs parts or materials, they’re itemized on your quote so you can approve them before anything is ordered or fitted.
What about my files?
Mention it in your request if there’s anything you need kept, so I can plan around it before starting. If you can, keep your own backup of anything important too.
Why is a job quoted higher than the price list?
The listed prices are for straightforward jobs. If yours turns out to be more involved, for example a stubborn malware case, extra troubleshooting or moving files to a new drive, I quote the extra work separately and only do it if you agree.
Where do I drop it off?
Drop-offs are by appointment only. Once we’ve agreed on a time, I’ll send you the details directly.

Privacy notice

          
What I collect. What you enter in the repair request form: your name, email, phone number if you give one, your computer’s type, make, model and operating system, the service you want, a description of the problem, and whether files need keeping.

          
Why. Only to reply to you, arrange a drop-off and carry out the repair. I don’t use it for marketing, I don’t send newsletters, and I don’t sell your information.

          
How it gets to me. Your request is delivered to my inbox by email, or through a form-handling service that forwards it there. Those email and form service providers process your submission only so it can be delivered to me. Apart from that, I don’t share your information with anyone.

          
How long I keep it. Only as long as it’s needed for your repair and any follow-up questions about it.

          
Your computer’s contents. I only access personal files when necessary for the requested repair. I don’t intentionally browse, copy or share unrelated personal content.

          
This website. It doesn’t use cookies, analytics or tracking.

          
Questions or deletion. Email me any time to ask what I have or to have it deleted.
